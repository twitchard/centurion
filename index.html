        Centurion Chess

        How to play
        • You and your opponent are playing 100 games of chess at once: you are white in 50 and black in 50.
        • All games appear stacked on one board. Each chip shows a piece and how many games have it on that square — a light chip P 34 means a white pawn sits there in 34 games.
        • Your black games are shown flipped so your pieces always start nearest you. One arrow speaks for both colors: e2→e4 also means e7→e5 in your black games.
        • On your turn, place one arrow: tap its start square, then its end square. Arrows are permanent and shared — they can move your opponent's games too.
        • After each arrow, every game advances one half-move. Each arrow ever placed (newest first) moves one random game where it is a legal move; every other game plays Stockfish's best move. Placing the same arrow again stacks it, doubling its pull.
        • Checkmate scores a point for the player whose side delivered it. Draws score nothing. Finished games leave the board.
        • You win when your lead is bigger than the number of games still running. If everything finishes level, the match is a draw.
        • Solo mode: you place every arrow. After each one, both half-moves play out back to back — no opponent arrows, just you steering 100 Stockfish games.
